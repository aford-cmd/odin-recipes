Christmas Sweater

Cozy yuletide sour

If an amaretto sour and a Christmas orange had a baby, it would be this delightful concoction.

Modesty, Christmas 2025

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1/2 oz amaretto
  • Dash of orange bitters
  • Splash of sour mix (Recipe below)
  • Thin slices of orange for ice cubes

Steps

  1. Fold up thin slices of oranges and place each in a large ice cube tray. Fill with water and freeze
  2. Add an orange ice cube to lo-ball glass
  3. Add gin, amaretto, bitters and sour mix
  4. Stir gently and serve

Sour mix

  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup lemon juice
  • 3/4 cup lime juice
  1. Heat water and sugar until dissolved
  2. Remove from heat and add juices

Sour mix can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
