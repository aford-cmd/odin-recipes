Reindeer Mule

Rudolph Goes to Russia

The classic Moscow Mule gets a holiday twist with cinnamon and cloves.

Tanya and Alan, Christmas 2025

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1/2 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz cinnamon simple syrup (Recipe below)
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • top with dark, spiced ginger beer
  • mint leaves
  • cinnamon stick
  • copper mugs for serving

Steps

  1. Fill copper mug with ice
  2. Add vodka, lime juice, and cinnamon syrup
  3. Top with ginger beer
  4. Add dashes of Angostura bitters
  5. Add several mint leaves
  6. Stir gently
  7. Garnish with cinnamon stick

Cinnamon Simple Syrup

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3-4 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 star anise
  • 2 cloves
Steps
  1. Bring water and sugar to a simmer
  2. Add cinnamon sticks and spices
  3. Simmer 5 - 10 minutes
  4. Remove from heat, let steep for 20 more minutes
  5. Strain and bottle

Cinnamon simple syrup can be kept in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
