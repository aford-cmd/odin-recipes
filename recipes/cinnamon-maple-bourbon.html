Cinnamon Maple Bourbon

Christmas Whiskey Drink

Gather round the fireplace with this cozy cocktail for chilly nights

Kara and Todd, Christmas 2025

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz lemon juice
  • 1/2 oz maple syrup
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • Cinnamon stick for garnish

Steps

  1. Add bourbon, lemon juice, maple syrup, and cinnamon to a shaker full of ice
  2. Shake and pour over ice
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick
