Rosemary Martini

Christmas tree in a glass

Two different gins and muddled rosemary give delightful herbal notes to this crisp, Christmasy cocktail. A splash of orange bitters and a twist of orange peel finish this off beautifully.

Angela and Jason, Christmas 2025

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Bombay Sapphire gin
  • 1 oz Monkey 47 gin
  • 1/2 oz vermouth
  • splash of orange bitters
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary, 1 for muddling, 1 for garnish
  • 1 piece orange peel, 2 inches long, twisted
  • cubed ice

Steps

  1. Chill martini glass in the refrigerator or freezer
  2. Gently muddle rosemary sprig in a cocktail shaker
  3. Add ice, gins, vermouth and bitters to the shaker
  4. Shake vigorously for several seconds
  5. Strain martini into the chilled glass
  6. Garnish with a sprig of rosemary and a twist of orange peel
